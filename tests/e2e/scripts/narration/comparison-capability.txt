Pimcore Comparison puts two data objects of the same class side by side.
Every field is diffed by a type-aware comparator. Text shows a word-level
inline diff. Relations are classified as added, removed, kept, or reordered.
Localized fields compare per language, so translation gaps stand out. Field
collections, bricks and the classification store nest as sections, and fields
you may not see are withheld. Filter to differences, then export to Excel or
JSON. Everything is computed server-side, permissions enforced, nothing persisted.
